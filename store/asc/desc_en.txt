Start in a street match, catch the scouts' eye and rise from the academy to the biggest clubs in the world. Write your own football career!

StarFoot is a football career game where you play the key moments of every match. The match runs minute by minute and pauses when the chance falls to you: the shot, the pass, the dribble, the free kick, the penalty or the header is in your hands.

YOUR CAREER
• Start in any year from 1980 to today, aged 8 to 18
• Impress scouts in street matches and academy trials
• Climb through the academy and sign your first pro contract
• Improve 9 attributes with training and mini-games

LEAGUES AND CUPS
• 400+ clubs in 30 leagues across Europe, the Americas, Asia and the Middle East
• Domestic cups and continental cups with a league phase and knockouts
• World Cup, European Championship, Nations League and youth national teams
• Golden Boot, the race for the world's best player award and end-of-season awards

TRANSFERS
• Weigh offers in the summer and winter windows: wage, contract length and your role
• Hire an agent and request your favourite shirt number

LIFE OFF THE PITCH
• Social media followers and sponsorship deals
• Family, relationships and nightlife: every choice has consequences
• Newspaper headlines, press conferences and a rival who chases you all career

AND MORE
• Goal celebrations, captaincy, man of the match and nearly 100 achievements
• Scenarios, generation mode and the Hall of Fame
• 5 career slots and 3 difficulty levels
• Available in 9 languages

All club and player names are fictional.
