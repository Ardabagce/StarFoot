## Resolution Center yanıtı (İngilizce, olduğu gibi yapıştır)

Hello App Review team,

Thank you for reviewing StarFoot. Please find the requested information below. The screen recording (captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS) is attached to this message.

**1. Screen recording**
Attached. It starts with launching the app from the Home Screen and shows the typical flow: language selection, App Tracking Transparency prompt, creating a new career, the weekly training/activities plan, playing a match, the league table and fixtures, European cup competitions, the shop/life section, an optional rewarded ad, and deleting a career save.
- StarFoot has **no account registration or login**. All progress is stored locally on the device. A career save can be deleted at any time from the main menu (trash icon on the save slot) or from Profile › Career › Game.
- StarFoot has **no user-generated content**. The in-game "social media" feed is entirely simulated by the game (posts are generated text and drawings); players cannot post, share or see content from other real users.
- StarFoot has **no paid content and no In-App Purchases**. All in-game money is virtual and earned only by playing. Optional rewarded ads give small in-game bonuses.

**2. Purpose and target audience**
StarFoot is a single-player, offline football (soccer) career simulation game. The player creates a young footballer, trains him week by week, plays matches through quick interactive moments, earns transfers to bigger clubs, competes in domestic leagues, national cups, continental club competitions and international tournaments, and manages the player's off-pitch life. It is aimed at adult (17+) football fans who enjoy career/management games and want a short-session game they can play anywhere, without an internet connection or an account. It provides entertainment and the experience of building a football career from a street player to a legend.

**3. How to access the main features**
No login, credentials or sample files are needed.
1. Launch the app, choose the language (Türkçe / English) at the top right if desired.
2. Tap "New Career", enter any name, choose nationality, position and difficulty, then tap "Start Career".
3. Play the street match / trial games (or skip them) to be signed by a club.
4. On the Home tab, use the Weekly Plan (training and activities), then tap "Play Match". Matches can be played interactively or simulated with the "Simulate Match" button.
5. The League tab shows the table, fixtures, top scorers and cup competitions; Squad, Life (shop, lifestyle) and Profile (career history, achievements, settings) tabs are available from the bottom bar.
6. To delete a save: Main menu › trash icon on the save slot.

**4. External services**
- Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) — rewarded/interstitial advertising.
- Google User Messaging Platform (part of the AdMob SDK) — consent form for users in regions that require it (EEA/UK, US states).
- Apple App Tracking Transparency — permission request before personalised ads.
The app has no backend server, no authentication service, no payment processor, no analytics SDK and no AI service. Game data never leaves the device. The privacy policy is hosted at https://starfoot.vercel.app/privacy.html.

**5. Regional differences**
The app functions identically in all regions. The only differences are: the interface language follows the device language (Turkish or English, switchable in the app), and the Google consent form is shown only in regions where it is legally required.

**6. Regulated industry / third-party material**
StarFoot does not operate in a regulated industry. All clubs, players, leagues and competitions are fictional or parody names created for the game; the app uses no official logos, crests, kits, player likenesses, photos or trademarks of any league, club or federation, and all visuals are drawn by the game's own code. The game contains simulated casino/betting mini-features that use only virtual in-game currency; there is no real-money gambling, no purchase of in-game currency and no cash-out. The age rating reflects this (Frequent/Intense Simulated Gambling).

Best regards,
Arda Bağçe

## Guideline 2.1 – App Tracking Transparency (8 Ekim 2026, build 1.0.2 (108))

### Ne düzeltildi
- ATT izni artık uygulama etkinleşir etkinleşmez, **reklam SDK'sı başlamadan ve hiçbir veri toplanmadan önce** isteniyor (`SceneDelegate.sceneDidBecomeActive` → ana iş parçacığında `ATTrackingManager.requestTrackingAuthorization`).
- Önceki sürümde istek, Google onay (UMP) sorgusundan sonra aynı hata bloğunda yapılıyordu; UMP sorgusu hata verdiğinde pencere hiç açılmıyordu. Ayrıca sahne tabanlı yaşam döngüsünde `applicationDidBecomeActive` çağrılmadığı için yerel istek de yoktu.
- JS tarafı (`js/admob.js`) ATT yanıtlanana kadar AdMob'u başlatmıyor.

### Ekran kaydı (fiziksel iPhone ile)
1. Ayarlar › Gizlilik ve Güvenlik › İzleme › **"Uygulamaların İzleme İsteğinde Bulunmasına İzin Ver" AÇIK** olmalı (kapalıysa pencere hiçbir uygulamada çıkmaz).
2. StarFoot'u telefondan **sil**, TestFlight'tan yeni build'i (109 ve üstü) kur.
3. Kontrol Merkezi › Ekran Kaydı'nı başlat.
4. Uygulamayı aç → yaklaşık 1 saniye içinde **"StarFoot'un sizi diğer şirketlerin uygulamaları ve web siteleri genelinde izlemesine izin verilsin mi?"** penceresi çıkar.
5. "İzin Ver" ya da "Uygulamadan İzleme İsteğinde Bulunmamasını İste"ye bas → (AB'deysen Google onay formu) → oyunun ana menüsü → Yeni Kariyer ile birkaç saniye oyna.
6. Kaydı durdur; videoyu App Review mesajına yanıt olarak ve **App Review Information › Notes / Attachment** alanına ekle.

### Apple'a yanıt (İngilizce, kopyala-yapıştır)

Hello,

Thank you for the review. We found and fixed the issue: the App Tracking Transparency request was made after an ad-consent check, and on some devices that check failed before the request was shown.

In the new build, the ATT permission request is presented as soon as the app becomes active on first launch, on the main thread, before the Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized and before any data that could be used for tracking is collected. Ads are only initialized after the user responds to the ATT prompt.

Attached is a screen recording on a physical iPhone showing:
1. A fresh install of the app,
2. The App Tracking Transparency permission request appearing immediately on first launch, before any tracking data is collected,
3. The user flow after the request (the main menu and starting a new career).

The new build has been submitted for review with this version. Thank you!

## 1.0.3 gönderimi: App Review Information › Notes (İngilizce, kopyala-yapıştır) — Premium'suz sürüm

StarFoot is a single-player football career game. No account or login is required; all game data is stored on the device.

App Tracking Transparency: On first launch the ATT permission request is shown about one second after the app opens, before the Google Mobile Ads SDK is initialized and before any data that could be used for tracking is collected. A screen recording on a physical iPhone is attached.

Ads: Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are optional (the player chooses to watch one for an in-game reward). Interstitial ads appear at most twice per season (season start and mid-season), never in the player's first season.

In-app purchases: This version has no in-app purchases.

Simulated gambling: The Life tab contains an optional fictional casino and sports betting section, available only to players aged 18+ in the game. It uses in-game currency only; no real money can be wagered, in-game currency cannot be bought, and nothing can be cashed out.

All club, league and player names are fictional parodies; no real club logos, kits or likenesses are used.
