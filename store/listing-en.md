Start in a street match at age 8, catch the scouts' eye, rise from the academy to the first team and become a legend at the biggest clubs in the world!

StarFoot is a football career game where you play the key moments of every match. The game runs minute by minute and pauses when the chance falls to you: the shot, the pass, the dribble, the free kick, the penalty or the header is in your hands.
